Four Leading KPI Dimensions

The signals that predict failure.
Before it reaches production.

Dimension 01, Primary Indicator
On-Time Delivery
Max 35 points, weighted most heavily
The strongest single predictor of a supplier in distress. Decline in OTD precedes quality issues and stockouts by 2–3 weeks. Thresholds: <70% = 35 pts, 70–84% = 20 pts, 85–91% = 8 pts. Used as the primary triage signal.
Industry-standard Tier 1 practice
Dimension 02, Quality Signal
Defect Rate
Max 30 points, triggers SCAR workflow
Rising defect rate signals process instability before delivery failures appear. Above 4% triggers SCAR (Supplier Corrective Action Request) workflow. Thresholds: >4% = 30 pts, 2–4% = 18 pts, 1–2% = 8 pts.
SCAR escalation alignment
Dimension 03, Production Risk
Inventory Coverage
Max 25 points, immediate line risk
Below 7 days of coverage means a single delivery miss stops the production line. Thresholds: <5 days = 25 pts (immediate risk), 5–9 days = 14 pts, 10–13 days = 6 pts. Most actionable near-term indicator.
Days-of-coverage model
Dimension 04, Process Stability
Lead Time Variance
Max 10 points, instability signal
High variance even when average lead times look acceptable is a signal of operational instability. A supplier averaging 18 days with 50% variance is less reliable than one averaging 25 days with 8% variance. Thresholds: >40% = 10 pts, 20–40% = 5 pts.
Process stability proxy

Consultant Verdict
REAL PROBLEM $260K/hr production stoppage cost is a real, cited Aberdeen Research benchmark, credible and defensible in interviews.

SOUND SCORING The 4-KPI model mirrors supplier evaluation frameworks used by Tier 1 automotive manufacturers. OTD as the primary indicator (35 pts max) is correct industry practice. SCAR trigger at 4% defect rate aligns with AIAG standards.

WATCH Scoring thresholds are industry-aligned heuristics, not statistically derived from a dataset. If challenged: "Calibrated to Tier 1 manufacturing benchmarks; thresholds are configurable to your fleet's baseline."

NOTE The single-source +10 penalty is conservative. In a real model this warrants a separate risk flag, not just additive scoring. Consider adding a separate single-source alert overlay.
